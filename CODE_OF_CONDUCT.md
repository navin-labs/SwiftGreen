# Code of conduct

SwiftGreen is maintained by one person in public. Everyone who takes part (issues, pull requests, discussions) agrees to this:

- **Be decent.** Disagree with ideas, never attack people. No harassment, slurs, sexual content, or personal attacks, and no publishing someone's private information.
- **Assume good faith, then be specific.** "This crashes on Android 13 when…" helps; "this app is garbage" doesn't.
- **Respect people's time.** Search existing issues first, fill in the template, and follow up when asked.
- **No exploit details in public.** Security reports go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit, hide or lock anything that breaks these rules, and block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem with someone's conduct, email the maintainer (address on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/navin-labs)) or use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature. Reports are kept confidential.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which applies where this page is silent.
